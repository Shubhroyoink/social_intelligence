# Executive Summary

This report analyzes social media intelligence regarding the topic **"AI Agents"** across a dataset of 1,124 total posts. Conversation is concentrated on Telegram (557 posts) and YouTube (505 posts), with minor activity on X (62 posts). The landscape is characterized by dominant neutral-to-positive reception, centered on educational video content.

# Sentiment

The leading sentiment is **positive** at 48.2% (542 posts), closely followed by **neutral** at 46.4% (522 posts). Negative sentiment is minimal at 5.3% (60 posts). 
* **YouTube** is the most positive platform (78.6% positive, 18.0% neutral, 3.4% negative).
* **X** shows a strong positive bias (59.7% positive, 35.5% neutral, 4.8% negative).
* **Telegram** is overwhelmingly neutral (73.4% neutral, 19.4% positive, 7.2% negative).
