### Executive Summary
This report analyzes social media intelligence surrounding "AI Agents" across 579 total posts on YouTube (333) and Telegram (246). The conversation is overwhelmingly constructive, characterized by high optimism on video platforms and objective, technical discussion in messaging channels.

### Sentiment
Positive sentiment dominates the discourse at 57.2% (331 posts), followed by neutral at 38.2% (221 posts), and minimal negative sentiment at 4.7% (27 posts).
*   **YouTube**: Highly optimistic, with 83.5% positive, 14.1% neutral, and only 2.4% negative sentiment.
*   **Telegram**: Marginally cautious and information-focused, registering 70.7% neutral, 21.5% positive, and 7.7% negative sentiment.

### Emotion & Stance
Emotionally, the landscape is highly objective, with "neutral" leading at 382 counts. Where emotion is expressed, "joy" (127) and "surprise" (34) significantly outpace negative indicators like "fear" (15) and "anger" (11).
*   **Stance**: Most posts maintain a neutral stance (508), with 43 supportive and 28 against.
*   **Sarcasm**: Sarcasm is low, accounting for only 4.1% of the total dataset.

### Trends
The conversation revolves around technical developments and active research.
*   **Top Keywords**: "engineering" (frequency: 5), "astra" (4), "cognitive" (3), "visual" (3), and "future" (3).
*   **Rising Terms**: "openai", "engineering", "says", "astra", and "people" represent the fastest-growing topics of interest.

### Demographics
*   **Languages**: English is the dominant language by a wide margin (538 posts), followed by Italian (7), Afrikaans (4), and Indonesian (4).
*   **Geography**: China leads geolocated posts (10), followed by Germany (3), Japan (2), the USA (2), and London/UK (2).
*   **Interests**: Audiences are primarily interested in general topics (294), AI/ML (240), Technology (99), and Education (50).

The "AI Agents" discourse is highly favorable, driven by optimistic YouTube content and research-focused discussions about systems like Google's "Astra" and cognitive engineering.
