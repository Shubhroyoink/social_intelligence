### Executive Summary

This report analyzes social media intelligence surrounding the topic of **AI Agents** across 644 total posts, spanning YouTube (401 posts) and Telegram (243 posts). The overall discourse is highly positive and driven primarily by English-speaking tech and AI/ML enthusiasts, with YouTube serving as the primary hub for optimistic discussions.

### Sentiment

The dominant sentiment across the dataset is **positive**, accounting for 58.5% (377 posts), followed by neutral at 36.8% (237 posts), and negative at a minor 4.7% (30 posts). Platform dynamics show a stark contrast:
*   **YouTube**: Strongly positive (80.5% positive, 15.7% neutral, 3.7% negative).
*   **Telegram**: Moderately neutral (71.6% neutral, 22.2% positive, 6.2% negative).

### Emotion & Stance

The prevailing emotional tone is **neutral** (407 counts). Among expressed emotions, **joy** leads significantly with 150 counts, followed by **surprise** (45), while negative emotions like fear (15), anger (11), sadness (10), and disgust (6) remain low. 
*   **Stance counts**: 562 neutral, 51 supportive, and 31 against.
*   **Sarcasm**: Minimal, measured at 4.3%.

### Trends

Discussions focus heavily on simulated environments and specific brand developments. 
*   **Top Keywords**: "agents" (9), "astra" (5), followed by "cybercab" (2), "living" (2), and "simulated" (2).
*   **Rising Terms**: "agents", "astra", "own", "agent", and "openai".
